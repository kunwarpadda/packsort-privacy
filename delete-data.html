Delete PackSort account data

PackSort accounts are created and managed by the PackSort administrator. You can request deletion even though registration is not available inside the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email kunwarpadda46@gmail.com with the subject “PackSort data deletion request”.
  2. Include the email address used to sign in to PackSort.
  3. The administrator will verify the request and delete the associated Firebase Authentication account.
Request deletion

What is deleted

The administrator deletes the Firebase Authentication account and its associated authentication identifier. Google removes live and backup authentication data according to Firebase's published retention schedule.

Data stored on the device

Local scan history and routing information are removed by clearing PackSort's app data or uninstalling the app. If the device is employer-managed, the device administrator can also remove local app data.
